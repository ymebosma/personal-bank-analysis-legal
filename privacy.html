Privacy notice

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Personal Bank Analysis is a private, non-commercial application operated by an individual solely to analyse payment accounts belonging to that same individual.

Data processed

The application may retrieve account identifiers, account details, balances and transaction information after the account holder explicitly authorises access through their bank. It does not initiate payments.

Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed only to provide the account holder with a consolidated personal financial analysis. Processing is based on the account holder's explicit request and consent.

Storage and sharing

Retrieved data is stored in private systems controlled by the account holder. It is not sold, used for advertising or made available to the public. Data is shared only with service providers required to establish the authorised account-information connection, or where required by law.

Retention and security

Data is retained only while needed for the personal analysis and is protected with reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Access credentials and private keys are kept outside public source repositories.

Your choices

The account holder can revoke bank consent through the relevant bank or connection provider and can request deletion of locally stored data. Privacy questions can be sent to mail@yme.nl.
